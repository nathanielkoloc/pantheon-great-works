The Opportunity

The world needs Great Works.
No one is building them.

Philanthropy funds incrementalism. Venture capital funds companies. There is no vehicle for the ambitious, world-shaping project — the kind of thing that requires vision, patience, and a patron who wants to leave something behind, not just a tax receipt. Great Works Studio fills that gap.

"You know greatness when you see it — it has soul. It's the manifestation of one human's unique vision of the world."

What We Do

We concept, fund, and execute

The Business Model

Two compounding revenue streams.

Stream 1 — Patron Admin Fees: Every project carries a 10% administration fee to the Studio. A $10M project generates $1M in net revenue. Immediate, recurring, high-margin.

Stream 2 — The Great Works License: Every project signs a license (modeled on the SAFE note) granting GWS 10% equity participation in any future economic value. 30% of our portfolio is commercially-oriented. As stakes accumulate, we build a permanent capital vehicle — with GWS charging ~1% management fee on AUM.

The Patron Approach

Not philanthropy — self-actualization.

We cultivate patron relationships personally and over time, approaching UHNWI and family offices from the standpoint of legacy, not charity. Phase I scoping engagements run $100–500K; implementation budgets are project-specific ($1M–$10M+). Our founding team brings 5+ years of direct family office experience across 4–5 offices.
